About: I am a third-year stat­istics PhD stu­dent at the Univ­ersity of Wisc­onsin–Mad­ison rece­iving acad­emic guid­ance from Prof­essor Joshua Cape.

Research interests: I study statis­tically and mathem­atically prin­cipled methods of dimens­ionality reduc­tion and struc­ture disc­overy for high-dimens­ional and comp­lex data.
